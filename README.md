# LIF100-Data-Project
A data analysis project using Excel, charts, claims, evidence and limitations.

My project is about the analyzation of data regarding the student usage of AI.
My driving question was : Do students use AI more for academic or personal purposes?
I collected data using google surveys containing a series of questions and the data collection period was over the span of 3 days. I surveyed a total of 102 students. 
My main claiim is that students primarily use AI as a dual-purpose tool, it has seamlessly integrated in both academic and personal life decisions of a student, but students tend to favor the use of it for academics. One number that supports this is 40.2% of the respondents are sometimes likely to study, 17.6% oftenly use it to study and 12.7% always use it to study. 
One limitation that I enocountered when working on this project was the stigma around the usage of AI may lead to innacurate responses collected. Especially in a school setting it was important that the data would be collected through google surveys without collecting emails in order to maintain respondents anonymous thus getting them to respond honestly.
